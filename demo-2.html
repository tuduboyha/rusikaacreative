Send it right the first time

A few habits that make your print come out sharp.

Photos

Send the original file from your phone or camera, not a screenshot or a forwarded copy. The bigger the file, the sharper the print.

Logos

Send a PNG or PDF of your logo if you have one. If you only have a photo of it, send that and we will tell you if it works.

Text

Type names, dates and phone numbers out in the chat so we can copy them exactly. Check the spelling once before you send.

Quick answers

Do I have to pay online?

No. There is no sign-up or online payment on this site. You chat with us on WhatsApp, and we confirm how and when you pay before we start.

Will I see the design before it is printed?

Yes. We share a design preview first, and we print only after you approve it.

Can you make something that is not listed?

We make most items to order. Choose "Something else" in the builder, or message us what you need.

Do you deliver?

You can pick up from our shop. For delivery, ask us on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is possible for your area.
