FAQs

Quick answers before you call

How do I place an order?

Call us or send a message on WhatsApp with what you need. We reply with a design preview and price, and make it once you approve.

Will I see the design before it is printed?

Yes. We share a design preview first, and we print only after you approve it.

Do I need to pay online?

No. There is no sign-up or online payment on this site. We confirm how and when you pay on WhatsApp before we start.

Can you make something that is not listed?

We make most items to order, so please ask. Message us what you need and we will tell you if we can make it.

Do you deliver, or do I have to pick up?

You can pick up from our shop near EGL Chowk, Sarjamda. For delivery, ask us on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is possible for your area.

What photo or logo should I send?

Send a clear JPG or PNG, as high quality as you have, in the same WhatsApp chat. A logo as a PNG or PDF is best. We will tell you if it needs changes.
