Pick it up from our shop, or ask us about delivery. Here is how it works.

Every product at Rusikaa Creative is made to order. There are two ways to get your order: pick it up from our shop, or ask us about delivery. We agree the details with you on WhatsApp before you approve the order.

4. How long it takes

We start making your order once you have approved the design and the payment terms have been agreed. How long it takes depends on the item, the quantity and the design. We give you an estimated ready date along with the price, before you approve the order.

Wedding seasons, festivals and exam time can be busy, so please order early and tell us the date you need it by.

Ready dates are our best estimates. If something delays your order, we will tell you as soon as we know, and we will agree a new date with you.
